I have always been fascinated by interactive and attractive technologies mostly websites, and yet the MERN stack had always felt like a nightmare to me, something I admired from a distance but was too afraid to approach.

And then this Internship opportunity came. Initially, I thought this internship would be about Python. But later I came to know that it was about MERN Stack…and honestly, something felt different this time. I didn’t feel fear. Instead, I felt a spark... a spark to finally learn something I had always admired but never had the courage to start.

You might be wondering where this courage came from. For me, it started during the inaugural meeting, where we got to interact with the Pinternship team. They told us that before moving into projects, we would first learn MERN through their own platform. That made me feel relieved. Knowing that beginners like me were learning alongside people who were already experienced made the journey feel less intimidating.

The very first thing we have to do is to learn MERN from ViBe Platform. It is the first time I used such a learning platform which requires your full attention while learning…remember the time when you have to stay silent at the Physical classroom…it is just like that or I would say exactly like that. And that is where I noticed that my attention span has increased. And the quiz part beautifully implements the concept of active recall. The Dashboard also provides a healthy competitive environment to keep track of our progress. And the fear of falling behind whenever I see my rank in dashboard is the motivation of what pushed me forward to be consistent and honest.

The standups and live sessions are one of the best parts of this internship. Every time I attend them, it feels like stepping into a space full of inspiring people.

I’m thankful to the Sudarshan Iyengar Sir, Vicharanashala Lab for Education Design, ANNAM.AI and mentors for shaping this experience with care. I am also thankful to NPTEL and Indian Institute of Technology, Ropar for this opportunity.
